## Verdict

**Ready for user acceptance.** M15 criterion V03 is **met**. Epic #1009 stays **open** until the user accepts it.

`filtfilt` zero-phase filters with Gustafsson initial conditions. `butter` now designs a two-edge bandpass beside the existing lowpass and highpass. `bandpass(x,[f1 f2],fs)` is that design at order 2, applied with `filtfilt`. The record-100 story filters MLII at 0.5–40 Hz and plots the first 10 s of raw and filtered on one axes.

## 中文摘要

记录 100 的 MLII 用 2 阶 Butterworth、`filtfilt` 做 0.5–40 Hz 零相位带通。薄封装 `bandpass` 走同一条设计，两条结果的最大绝对差是 0。前 10 秒的原始波形和滤波波形画在同一张坐标轴上（2 条线）。滤波后均值约 0（测得 -0.000010 mV），标准差从 0.193200 mV 到 0.179714 mV。Epic #1009 保持打开。

## Functional demo (real CLI output)

```text
$ openmatlab -e "run('demos/mitbih/story_record100.m')"
MIT-BIH record 100 Fs=360 channels=2 samples=650000 MLII_mV0=-0.145000
V03 band=0.5-40 order=2 nlines=2 mean=-0.000010 std_raw=0.193200 std_filt=0.179714 agree=0.000e+00
```

`agree` is `max(abs(filtfilt(b,a,x) - bandpass(x,[0.5 40],fs)))` on the full MLII column. The figure is the first 3600 samples (10 s), not all 650000 points. The 0.5 Hz and 40 Hz edges are the half-power points of one `butter` pass; `filtfilt` squares that magnitude.
