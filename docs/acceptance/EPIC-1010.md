## Verdict

**Ready for user acceptance.** M15 criteria V04 and V05 are **met**. Epic #1010 stays **open** until the user accepts it.

`findpeaks` on the 0.5–40 Hz MLII column uses `MinPeakHeight` 0.5 mV and `MinPeakDistance` 90 samples (0.25 s). RR is the difference of those locations in seconds, drawn as a histogram in milliseconds. The amplitude spectrum is `abs(fft)` of the first 10 s. `.atr` is not the detector.

## 中文摘要

滤波后的 MLII 用 `findpeaks`（0.5 mV、至少间隔 0.25 s）标出 R 峰。测得 2273 个峰，平均 RR 0.794594 s，前 10 秒 13 个峰。RR 直方图和 10 秒片段的幅度谱各有一张图。谱峰在 1.200000 Hz。演示窗上的峰与固定局部极大规则一致（容差 2 个采样点以内）。`.atr` 不是金标准。Epic #1010 保持打开。
